GET TO KNOW YOUR MODPOD

INFORMATION

CARRY YOUR OWN WAY

CARRY YOUR OWN WAY WITH MODULAR PODS FOR YOUR CASH, CARDS, AND SMALL ESSENTIALS.

EVERY LEFT POD IS COMPATIBLE WITH EVERY RIGHT POD, REGARDLESS OF SIZE. JUST PULL THE JOINT PIN AND SWAP BETWEEN THEM.

EACH CARD-CARRYING POD INCLUDES REPLACEABLE WEAR INSERTS. WHEN THE PLASTIC WEARS DOWN, POP THE OLD ONES OUT WITH THE JOINT PIN, CUT THE LONG, INCLUDED PLASTIC ROD TO THE SAME SIZE, AND INSERT INTO THE SLOT.

NOTE: NEW PLASTIC INSERTS ARE TIGHT!

MODELS COME OUT ALL THE TIME, SO CHECK BACK TO SEE WHAT’S NEW!
